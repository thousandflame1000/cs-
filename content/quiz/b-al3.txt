@q np4-01 | al-np#4 | 2 | 觀念
A minimization algorithm always returns a feasible solution and has approximation ratio 2 for nonnegative objective values. On one instance the optimum is 10, and candidate returned costs are 10,15,20,25. Apply the ratio guarantee to these values without treating it as an execution-time ratio or a probability of finding the exact optimum. For the corresponding maximization convention, the ratio compares optimum divided by returned value. Which statements are correct? Select all that apply.
[x] The solution it returns costs at most twice the optimal cost.
[x] On some inputs it may return an optimal solution.
[x] For a maximization problem, ratio 2 would mean the returned value is at least half of the optimal value.
[ ] Its running time is at most twice that of the fastest exact algorithm for the problem.
[x] For a minimization instance with optimum ten, any feasible returned cost between ten and twenty meets the ratio-two guarantee.
---
「成本至多兩倍最佳」對：這是最小化的品質保證。「有時恰好最佳」對：上界允許相等。「最大化至少一半」對：採通常對稱近似比定義時，最佳值除回傳值至多二。「執行時間比」錯：近似比比較目標值，不比較時間。「最佳十則十至二十合格」對：可行解不低於最優十，二倍上界為二十；它不表示恰有一半輸入被精確解出。

@q np4-02 | al-np#4 | 2 | 追蹤
點覆蓋的 2-近似：每次挑一條還沒被覆蓋的邊，把兩端都加進來。圖是一條路徑 1–2–3–4，依序挑邊 (1,2)、然後還沒被覆蓋的 (3,4)。得到的覆蓋大小？最佳是多少？
== 4,2
?? 近似,最佳
---
挑 (1,2) 加入 1、2；(2,3) 已被覆蓋；挑 (3,4) 加入 3、4。共 4。最佳 {2,3}，大小 2，剛好 2 倍。

@q np4-03 | al-np#4 | 3 | 證明
證明點覆蓋「挑邊加兩端」的近似比是 2。
---
設挑出的邊集合是 $A$。$A$ 中的邊兩兩沒有共同端點（挑了一條就把兩端加入，之後不會再挑碰到它們的邊）。任何點覆蓋都要覆蓋 $A$ 的每條邊，又不能用一個點覆蓋兩條，所以最佳解 $\ge|A|$。演算法輸出 $2|A|\le2\cdot$最佳。
