@q hp1-09 | ds-heap#1 | 2 | 觀念
The Build-Heap procedure below turns an arbitrary array `a[1..n]` into a max-heap, where `sift_down(a, i, n)` repeatedly swaps `a[i]` with its larger child until neither child is larger:
```
build_heap(a, n):
    for i = floor(n/2) downto 1:
        sift_down(a, i, n)
```
For example, with $n = 15$ the loop visits indices 7 down to 1: four nodes of height 1, two of height 2, and the root, of height 3. A student claims that the running time grows like $n\lg n$, because the loop runs about $n/2$ times and each call of `sift_down` can take about $\lg n$ steps. Which of the following statements are correct? Select all that apply.
[x] It runs in $O(n)$ time, because most nodes are near the bottom and can move down only a few levels.
[x] About $n/2^{h+1}$ nodes have height $h$, and each moves down at most $h$ levels, so the total work, added over all heights, is less than $n$.
[ ] It runs in $O(n)$ time because each node is compared only once.
[x] Inserting the $n$ elements one by one instead can take time proportional to $n\lg n$.
[x] Leaves never move down at all.
---
「大部分節點很矮」對：會沉很深的只有靠近根的少數幾個。

「$\sum h\cdot n/2^{h+1}<n$」對：這是嚴格的證明。

「每個只比較一次」錯：靠近根的要比較很多次。

「一個一個插入是 $n\lg n$」對：大部分點在底層、要往上爬很遠。

「葉子不用沉」對：Build-Heap 從最後一個內部節點 $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ 開始往前做，葉子本身就是合法的堆積。

「找第 $i+k$ 小」錯：跳過的 $k$ 個要扣掉，不是加上。

「程式沒錯」錯：右邊子陣列的排名從 1 重新算，原本的第 $i$ 小在那裡是第 $i-k$ 小。

「要用 $i-k+1$」錯：pivot 不在右邊，$k$ 已經把它算進去了。

@q srt5-13 | ds-sort#5 | 1 | 計算
9 個相異的數，中位數是第幾小？
== 5
---
$n$ 是奇數時中位數是第 $\frac{n+1}2$ 小：第 5 小，左右各 4 個。

@q srt5-14 | ds-sort#5 | 3 | 證明
用代入法證明：$T(n)\le T(n/5)+T(7n/10)+cn$ 的解是 $O(n)$。
---
猜 $T(n)\le dn$。代入：$T(n)\le d\frac n5+d\frac{7n}{10}+cn=\frac{9}{10}dn+cn$。要 $\le dn$，只要 $cn\le\frac{1}{10}dn$，即 $d\ge10c$。取 $d=10c$（並讓小的 $n$ 也成立）即得 $T(n)=O(n)$。關鍵是 $\frac15+\frac7{10}<1$。
